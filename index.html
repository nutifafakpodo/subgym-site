The problem

Ghana's fitness market runs on cash and guesswork

1

Gyms have no software

Memberships live in notebooks and WhatsApp. Renewals and churn are invisible.

2

No access hardware

Turnstiles are rare and expensive. There's no reliable way to verify who walks in.

3

Payments are offline

Members pay cash or mobile money, with no record tying a payment to a person.

4

Trainers & sellers are cut off

Personal trainers and supplement vendors have no channel to reach members.

Every gym is an island.

Every member relationship is offline — and everything sold around it is invisible to the gym. The result: no data, no retention tools, no way to grow revenue per member.

Subgym connects all of it.
The product

Four apps, one shared platform

Each app faces one side of the fitness economy. Behind them, SubSystem runs the whole platform from one console.

Sf

SubFit

Members · iOS & Android

Find gyms and book classes, subscribe or buy a day pass, check in with a QR code, follow trainer plans, and shop supplements and gear — with a wallet and family profiles.

Sg

SubGym

Gym owners & front desk · web

QR front-desk check-in, members and freezes, plans and pricing, classes, renewal reminders, referral codes and reports.

St

SubTrainer

Personal trainers · web & mobile

Build and sell structured workout & diet plans, publish them to subscriber groups, and track subscribers, ratings, earnings and payouts.

Sv

SubVendor

Marketplace sellers · web & mobile

List supplements, apparel, footwear and equipment with photos and variants; manage stock, fulfil orders, and run subscribe-and-save renewals.

Sy

SubSystem The platform console

Verification, feature flags, commissions and moderation — set once here, live in every app straight away. No redeploy, no app-store release.

How it works

The two-sided loop that makes it work

From discovery to the front desk in five steps — the core loop between members and gyms.

1

Discover

Member finds a nearby gym in SubFit.

2

Subscribe

Pays with mobile money or card.

3

Get a QR

A reusable code lands in the app.

4

Scan

The front desk scans it in SubGym.

5

Check in

Green = valid, with name & plan.

No turnstiles. A phone screen and a cheap barcode scanner is the entire access-control stack — validated in one shared service, so member, walk-in and family check-ins never drift apart.
The platform

More than an app — a complete platform

The pieces that turn four apps into one platform partners can trust.

QR check-in

Reusable member codes, single-use day passes and family QRs, validated at the door with a plain answer — admitted, or why not.

Feature flags

Turn any feature on or off across every app in real time — for one gym or the whole platform. No redeploy.

Commissions

Per-category rates set once, flowing read-only into every partner's earnings, with a full audit log.

Verification

Gyms, trainers and vendors stay invisible until an admin approves them. Nobody trades until a human says so.

Fitness marketplace

Trainer plans and a full store — supplements, apparel, footwear, equipment — with photos, variants, live stock and per-vendor fulfilment.

Freeze & family

Pause a membership when travelling; add family members, each with their own QR on one membership.
