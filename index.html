SubGym · for gym owners

Everything your gym needs — without the hardware

SubGym runs in the browser on any laptop or tablet. Your front desk, your members, your plans and your numbers, in one place.

QR front desk

Members show their SubFit pass; your staff scan it, or type the six-character code. A clear “admitted” with their photo — or the reason why not: expired, not started yet, or for another gym.

Plans & pricing

Walk-in day passes, monthly to annual plans, group memberships, and family memberships soon — each with your price and what’s included. Members can renew automatically by card, with a text 3 days before.

Members at a glance

Search the roster, see who ends this week, freeze a membership, and add or remove days with the reason on record. A frozen member’s next scan at the door unfreezes them and adds the paused days back.

Get found on SubFit

Your gym page shows photos, plans, classes and member reviews to people nearby — who can join and pay on the spot.

Renewal remindersComing soon

With one tap, text every member whose membership is due — in the week before it ends, on the day and just after — so fewer of them lapse.

Classes & bookingsComing soon

Schedule runs, yoga, HIIT or spin, free or paid. Members book a spot in SubFit; you see who’s coming. Cancel a class and every paid place is refunded for you.

Take payments at the desk

Sell a walk-in a day pass or a membership on the spot: send a mobile money prompt to their phone, or text a payment link. Once it’s paid, check them in straight away.

Referral rewardsComing soon

Members invite friends with their own code. You choose the reward: a free day pass for the member, or 5–50% off for both. It unlocks only once the friend has paid and checked in.

Earnings you can check

Every sale shows what the member paid, SubGym’s commission, the fee and your share — and when it’s ready to be paid out to your account.

Trust & safety

Safe for members, fair to partners

Members pay before they train, shop or book — so the platform has to be one they can trust, and one you can count on to pay you.

Everyone is checked

No gym, seller or trainer sells until a person at SubGym has checked them. If an approved partner changes their name, we check that too.

Payments through Paystack

Card and mobile money payments go through Paystack. SubGym never handles cash, and there’s no wallet to top up.

A rate that stays put

Each sale keeps the commission rate it was sold at, and you can see it next to every sale. Every rate change is logged.

Your payout account, protected

Account numbers are encrypted, and a new or changed payout account waits 72 hours before money goes to it — so nobody can quietly redirect your earnings.

The right access for each person

Front-desk staff check members in and take payments; revenue, plans and payouts stay with the owner. A member’s sign-in can’t open a business app.

Refunds handled

A cancelled class, a session cancelled before it starts, or an order a seller can’t fulfil — SubGym sorts out the member’s money back, so you don’t have to.

FAQ

Questions gyms, sellers and trainers ask

Do I need turnstiles or special hardware?

No. Your front desk opens SubGym in the browser on a laptop or tablet. Members show the QR pass in SubFit; staff scan it with a plug-in barcode scanner, or type the code.

What does it cost?

SubGym is commission-based: you pay a share of what you sell through the platform, with no hardware to buy. Ask us for the current rate for your gym or product category.

How do members pay?

In the SubFit app, by card or mobile money, paid upfront. A card can also renew a gym membership automatically, with a text 3 days before.

Can walk-ins pay at the front desk?

Yes. Staff pick a day pass or plan, type the customer’s number, and send a mobile money prompt or a payment link by text. Once it’s paid, they check the customer in on the spot — and the pass is waiting in SubFit when that person signs in.

Can my front-desk staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Front-desk staff see the overview, the front desk and members: they check members in, take payments and look members up. Revenue, plans, pricing, reports and payouts stay with the owner.

What if a member travels or gets injured?

The member can freeze their membership in SubFit, within your gym’s allowance, or you can freeze it for them. The next time they scan in at the desk it unfreezes, and the paused days are added to the end date. You can also add days with the reason on record.

When do I get paid?

Every sale shows in your earnings with SubGym’s commission and your share. Earnings are ready for payout a few days after the sale, and SubGym pays them to your mobile money or bank account. A new or changed payout account waits 72 hours first, to keep your money safe.

How do I get listed?

Gyms: book a demo and we set up your gym and owner account, then check your business documents. Sellers and trainers sign up in SubVendor or SubTrainer and send their business documents or certificates. Once we’ve checked them, you go live in SubFit.

I sell supplements and gear — do I need my own website?

No. SubVendor gives you a store inside SubFit, plus the tools to manage stock, fulfil orders and see your payouts.

What’s available now, and what’s coming soon?

The SubFit store, trainer plans and one-to-one sessions are coming soon, along with family memberships, and classes, renewal reminders, reports and referral rewards for gyms. Gyms can start now with memberships, day passes, check-in and desk payments. Book a demo now and we’ll get you checked and set up, ready for launch.

What if I can’t fulfil an order?

Tell us in SubVendor. SubGym takes it from there and sorts out the member’s order or refund.

I’m a personal trainer — how do I sell plans?

With SubTrainer. Set up your profile and send your certificates; once we’ve checked them, your plans go live in SubFit. Choose monthly access, a version to keep, or both, and set your own prices.

Can I train clients one-to-one?

Yes. Add the sessions you offer — in person at a place you choose, or by video call — with a length and price, and set your weekly hours. Members book and pay in SubFit; you mark each session done, and if you cancel before it starts, the member is refunded in full.

Do members see my plan before they pay?

They see what’s inside — the days or meals, the level and the length — plus ratings and reviews from members who have it. The plan itself only opens once they’ve bought it.

What keeps members going once they buy?

In SubFit, members start your plan on the days they choose and get a reminder each morning of what to do. They earn points, badges and streaks for every workout, and can go through your plan with friends — each of whom buys it too.
